A2 is a scale/history account. The final set favors broad, durable concepts and varied backend/frontend/DSA coverage while removing 40 duplicate, overly granular, setup-specific, or lower-priority candidates. Historical sources are deliberately useful content that a long-term learner could plausibly have removed from Library after studying, rather than fake unavailable videos.
